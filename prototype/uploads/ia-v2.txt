3 · Issues found and proposed fixes
Dead end — cash Summary shows “Cash Submitted ₹0.00” but no path to submit cash exists in the
captured flows. Proposed: a Cash submission screen reachable from Summary.
Orphaned targets Six taps lead to screens missing from the capture set: Earnings “View details”, Referral
flow, Premier League ranking, notification bell, hamburger menu, NSL “Others”
sub-reasons. Audit whether they exist or are unbuilt.
No persistent global
nav
FE home, Shipments and Summary are three hubs with no shared bottom nav; returning
Home from duty context is not visible. Proposed: fixed 3-tab bottom nav (Home /
Shipments / Summary) — the highest-impact fix for one-handed, on-road use.
Duplicated home Docs-pending home and activated home are one screen with two states — modelled as a
single stateful screen so design doesn't fork.
RVP gap RVP has a tab and a count but no captured pickup-execution flow — the reverse-pickup
counterpart of delivery is a mapping gap.
Field-driver principles
applied
NSL, POD and payment are modelled as states/branches of Order detail so a rider is
never more than 2 taps from Deliver, Collect or Mark undelivered; every blocking state
(geofence, call attempts) surfaces on the screen it blocks. Backlog: offline queue for POD
submissions; auto-advance to nearest next shipment after Complete.
